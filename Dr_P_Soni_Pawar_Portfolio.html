About me

An educator shaped by classrooms in three countries

Internationally experienced Commerce and Business academic with 22 years 10 months of teaching and leadership, of which over 14 years were spent abroad in the Republic of Maldives and Indonesia (2006 – 2020), followed by virtual lecturing for MIANZ International College, Maldives. Served as Dean, Senior Lecturer, Head of Department and Cambridge GCE/IGCSE O Level and AS & A Level teacher, and now Assistant Professor in India. Holds a Ph.D. in Commerce (Behavioural Finance) from Karpagam Academy of Higher Education (2026).

Experienced in undergraduate and postgraduate teaching, curriculum design, outcome-based education, student mentoring and international academic collaboration. Author of six Scopus-indexed book chapters (Taylor & Francis / Routledge) and active in faculty development, NPTEL certification and institutional administration.

Career objective: Contribute to excellence in higher education through innovative teaching, impactful research, academic leadership, and continuous professional development, while fostering critical thinking, ethical values, and lifelong learning among students.
